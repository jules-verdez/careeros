# Outreach templates

These are generic outlines, not verified candidate facts or approved messages. Fill brackets
only from reviewed CareerOS evidence, remove unsupported sentences, and respect local
do-not-contact rules. A local draft never grants permission to create or send external mail.

## Recruiter introduction (agency)

Subject: [role family] opportunities in [location]

Hi [first name],

I am looking for [role family] opportunities in [location]. My relevant experience includes
[one verified experience] and [one cited project or result].

Are you recruiting for roles that match this background?

[candidate name]

## Referral request (alumni or former colleague)

Subject: [Company] [role title]: would you be open to referring me?

Hi [first name],

I am considering [role title] at [company] ([canonical vacancy URL]). The work on
[specific requirement] matches [reviewed evidence].

Would you be comfortable referring me for this role?

[candidate name]

## Follow-up (7 days after an application or email, one bump only)

Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [first name],

A short follow-up on my [application / note] of [date] for [role]. I remain interested and
available from [date]. If the role is filled or paused, a one-line reply saves us both time.

Thanks,
[candidate name]
